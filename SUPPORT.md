# Mr.Blobsky Support

Mr.Blobsky is a macOS menu-bar toy that turns keyboard and trackpad play into music, sounds, and animated blob-cat stickers.

## Install

If you downloaded Mr.Blobsky directly, move it to the **Applications** folder before opening it and granting permissions. Open the app once, then follow the keyboard-access steps below. Keep the app in the same location afterward so macOS continues to recognize the permitted copy.

## Find and control the app

Mr.Blobsky has no Dock icon. Look for its cat icon in the menu bar.

- Press **⌥⌘K** to lock, and press **⌥⌘K** again to unlock.
- Press **⌥⌘T** to begin a two-minute play session.
- Press **⌥⌘1** through **⌥⌘5** to select a song.
- Press **⌥⌘P** to play the selected song.
- You can also use the same commands from the menu-bar menu.

## Allow keyboard access

Mr.Blobsky needs macOS **Accessibility** permission to keep playful key presses inside the app. Some macOS versions also request **Input Monitoring**.

1. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility**.
2. Turn on Mr.Blobsky.
3. If macOS also lists Mr.Blobsky under **Input Monitoring**, turn it on there too.
4. Quit and reopen Mr.Blobsky after changing a permission.

If permission is already on but the app still asks for it, turn Mr.Blobsky off in that list, remove it with the minus button if available, reopen the app, then add or enable the current copy again. Moving the app after granting permission can make macOS treat it as a different copy.

Until permission works, the play screen can appear while other apps still receive key presses. The menu always provides **Unlock**, and **⌥⌘K** remains the parent shortcut.

## Record or replace a sound

Choose **Record a Sound…** from the menu-bar menu. A new recording replaces the previous recording. The clip stays on this Mac and can be removed from the recording window.

Microphone access is optional; built-in sounds work without it. If recording does not begin, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone** and enable Mr.Blobsky.

## Two-minute play

After two minutes, sounds stop and Mr.Blobsky says **NOPE!**, but the keyboard stays contained so a parent remains in control. Press **⌥⌘K** or choose **Unlock** from the menu.

## Privacy

Mr.Blobsky has no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking, or network features. Read the full [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md).
